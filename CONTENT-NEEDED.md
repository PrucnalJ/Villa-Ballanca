# Content needed from Villa Ballanca

A homepage preview built from what is publicly on villaballanca.com as of 2026-09-06.

## Things on their current site they will want to know about

- **Every image is frozen at September 2021.** All assets load from
  `/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/`, and several are still using unedited export filenames —
  `New-Project-2021-09-16T144749.139.png` is doing duty as homepage imagery.
- **"Mission Statement" is a heading on a restaurant homepage.** It is template boilerplate
  ("committed to serving high-quality Italian meals... in a pleasant atmosphere") and it is
  the kind of thing a corporate intranet has, not a trattoria.
- **The copy is the GoDaddy template's, not theirs.** "Good Times. Good People. Great Food",
  "Treat Your Taste Buds", "Deliciousness in Every Bite", "Satisfy Your Craving" — none of it
  says anything a competitor could not also say.
- **The whole homepage is 34 lines of visible text.** For a restaurant with a dining room,
  a catering operation and banquet rooms, that is very little.

Worth saying plainly: **their copyright is current (© 2026) and their title tag is fine**
("Delicious Italian Food in Lower Burrell, PA | Villa Ballanca"). This is a thin site, not a
neglected one.

## The biggest gap: no menu content and no photographs

- **Neither menu is on the site as text.** "Our Menu" and "Catering To-Go Menu" are links.
  Nobody searching for a dish in Lower Burrell can find them, and nobody can read a menu
  comfortably on a phone.
- **There are no photographs of their food, their dining room or their banquet rooms.**
  For a restaurant this is the single most valuable thing to fix, ahead of everything else.

The preview does not invent either. It lists the two menus and describes the three sides of
the business, and leaves the actual dishes to them.

## Small things to confirm

- [ ] Banquet room capacity — how many rooms, and how many people each. Not stated anywhere,
      and it is the first question anyone booking will ask.

- [ ] Whether they still use Toast for ordering. Their site references it but no working
      ordering link was found, so nothing is linked on the preview.

## Conflict note for us, not for them

Two other food businesses have already been emailed: **Hoffstot's Cafe Monaco** (restaurant
and bar, Oakmont, ~8 miles) and **Jaden's Catering** (catering and banquets, Monroeville,
~10 miles). All three do banquets and catering.

They are different towns and different formats, so this is a soft conflict rather than a
direct one. But if any two of them compare notes, the "we picked a few local businesses we
actually like" framing is what suffers. Worth spacing the sends.

and warm cream, deliberately different from the black-and-gold used for Hoffstot's.
